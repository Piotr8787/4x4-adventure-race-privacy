Privacy Policy

4x4 Adventure Race is an Android racing game for Android.

1. User accounts

The game does not require registration or a user account.

2. Local game data

Race records, game settings and supported progress information may be saved locally on the user's device. The developer does not operate a server collecting these game records. Local data can generally be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the game, subject to device backup settings.

3. Google AdMob

The game integrates Google AdMob for advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, app interactions and diagnostic information. Approximate location may be inferred from the IP address.

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Consent

Where required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Available privacy options can be accessed through the AD PRIVACY feature when supported by the application.

5. Data retention

Locally stored records remain on the device until removed by the user or application. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention policies.

6. Permissions

The game does not require camera, microphone or precise location access for its gameplay. Internet connectivity may be used for advertising services.

7. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of their personal data, object to processing or withdraw consent.

8. Contact

Privacy inquiries: piotr.tuszynski@protonmail.com

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change.
