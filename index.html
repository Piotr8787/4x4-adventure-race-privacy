Privacy Policy

4x4 Adventure Race is a racing game for Android.

1. User accounts

The game does not require registration or a user account.

2. Local game data

Race records, game settings and supported progress information may be saved locally on the user's device. No external server is operated to collect these game records. Local data can generally be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the game, subject to device backup settings.

3. Google AdMob

The game integrates Google AdMob for advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs
  • IP addresses
  • Application interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Approximate location inferred from IP addresses

These data may be used for advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention and personalization where permitted.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising consent

Where required by applicable law, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Available privacy options can be accessed through the AD PRIVACY feature when supported by the application.

5. Data retention

Locally stored records remain on the device until removed by the user or application. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable data retention policies.

6. Permissions

The game does not require access to the camera, microphone or precise device location for gameplay. Internet connectivity may be used for advertising services.

7. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, object to processing or withdraw consent.

8. Contact

Privacy inquiries:

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data processing practices change.
